Northwind Mobile platform release notes, sprint 42. The checkout service now retries failed payment captures with an idempotency key, which removed the duplicate charges reported last month. Median API latency fell from 310 ms to 240 ms after the connection pool was resized. The notification service moved to a new SMS vendor; delivery receipts are now stored for 30 days. Known issue: the promotions cache can serve an expired offer for up to five minutes after it ends.
